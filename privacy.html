Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Ninety Garuda is an Indonesian football media brand. This policy explains what our publishing app does with data on Facebook and Instagram.

Who we are

Ninety Garuda runs the Ninety Garuda Facebook Page and related social accounts. The app is used only by our own team, to publish our own posts. It is not offered to the public, and nobody logs in to it.

What data we use

The app uses access tokens for our own accounts, so it can publish posts and read the performance of those posts, such as views and reach. We may also read publicly available posts from public football accounts, such as clubs and national teams, to follow the news.

What we don't collect

We don't collect personal data from people who see or interact with our posts through this app. We don't sell, rent or share personal data, and we don't use it for advertising.

Storage

Tokens and post performance data are stored privately on our own systems and are used only to run our publishing. Tokens are not shared with anyone.

Deleting your data

If you believe we hold data about you and want it deleted, email us at the address below. We'll respond and delete it within 30 days.
